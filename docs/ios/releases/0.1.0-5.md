# Reminduh 0.1.0 (5) — internal TestFlight beta

PR #2 was merged into main at `0f0a95cb1249e731ca692c18b3c9eba9bf90763e`.
Archive source: `fc38b87352f2fdd51b0526e8927364e7b604e9bc`.
Annotated tag: `ios/0.1.0-5`. Bundle: `com.reminduh.app`.

This build preserves pending reminders when their due time passes and retains
valid snoozed alerts. It also brings the refinements after build 4: compact Blobby
speech, clearer room and settings controls, improved larger-text layouts, sensory
activity controls, local music playback handling and reliable medication-field
focus after navigation.

Medication records, supply, default sadness and optional self-care remain separate.
The beta remains offline, with blank cloud endpoints, no development server and
disabled real-money purchases and Apple Music integration. No schema or backend
migration accompanies this release.

## Device checks still required

Update in place through TestFlight to preserve saved data. Validate actual iPhone
scheduled reminders and snoozes under Focus/restarts, audio routes and interruptions,
local-file playback ending/restarting, larger text and VoiceOver. Simulator and
browser success do not prove these physical-device conditions or explain a past
missed alert. Cloud sync and real purchases remain out of this beta's scope.
