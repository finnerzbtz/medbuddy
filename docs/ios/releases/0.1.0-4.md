Mobile shop selections open a focused details sheet, with a separate purchase
confirmation and reliable focus restoration. Quotes remain in Blobby's compact
speech bubble. Music and voice now show loading/failure feedback and retry controls;
late decoding cannot override pause or mute.

disabled. Existing medication behaviour, supply accounting, default sadness,
optional self-care separation and data persistence remain intact.
