# App Store Connect beta copy (draft)

## Beta description

Reminduh pairs a simple medication check-in routine with Blobby, a small companion with a room of their own. Add your schedule, get device reminders and record doses you have taken or skipped. Spend a quiet moment with Blobby: rake a sand garden, tend a bonsai, share a snack or decorate the room.

This first beta works on one device, with offline access and exportable backups. Games are optional. Calm display settings, reduced motion and independent audio controls help you make the experience comfortable.

## What to test

Please try adding a sample medication, enabling a test notification and receiving a scheduled reminder with the app closed. Check taken/skipped, snooze and undo. Explore Blobby's room, sound controls and calm settings, and try saving/restoring a backup through Files.

Tell us which iPhone/iOS version you use and the steps that caused any problem. Avoid sharing medication details or backup files in feedback screenshots.

Reminduh records your own check-ins; it does not provide medical advice. Follow your medication instructions and use your usual reminder method while testing this beta.

## Review notes

No sign-in is required. Start onboarding, enter optional display names, then add a sample medication or choose “I’ll add this later”. Notification permission is requested only after tapping Enable reminders under My Blobby. Test notification schedules an alert three seconds later.

All companion games and store items are optional. Scheduled check-ins earn 10 leaves each. Leaf purchases and Apple Music connection are currently disabled pending Apple service setup; do not advertise them as active in this beta. Blobby radio and local audio files are available through Sound → Open record player. There is no HealthKit, prescription verification or clinical decision functionality. All application assets and medication records work locally.

The app queues 60 alert times within a 30-day window and refreshes the queue when opened. A renewal notification asks the tester to return. Details and the scheduled-through date are visible in reminder settings.
